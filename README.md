# BioConnect

A single-page web app that helps students find and track bio-science placements across England (genomics, mycology, clinical research, health data and more) instead of the usual grad schemes.

Open `fringescience.html` in Chrome or Edge (keep `hero.mp4` next to it).

- **Dashboard**: stats, a live applications-by-status chart and upcoming deadlines
- **Internships**: searchable list of programs in England, filterable by North or South
- **Applications**: pipeline tracker (Prep essay → Submitted → Interview → Offer), saved in your browser
- **Interview prep**: pick a program to tailor everything to that company's role and values. Includes Q&A cards, a practice coach chat, and an AI mock interview: the interviewer reads each question aloud (text-to-speech), you toggle Record to answer, and your answer is transcribed (Web Speech API), scored, and paired with live facial expression reading (face-api.js). You choose when to move to the next question. Camera and mic are only on while recording.

- **CV check**: paste your CV and see which lab techniques recruiters look for are mentioned (4+ = great) and which are missing

Program deadlines and pay are sample estimates. Check each program's official site before applying.
